# 004 - Partner & Organization Management Module

## Overview
* **Status**: Pending
* **Milestone**: Core Business Services
* **Dependencies**: [002-user-and-access-management.md](002-user-and-access-management.md), [003-document-management.md](003-document-management.md)

---

## 1. Why the Feature Exists
Today, partner onboarding takes 5–10 days through fragmented emails, shared spreadsheets, and manual document collection. This module establishes a digital onboarding pipeline, profile management, approval workflows, and an agreement repository for external organizations (insurance/takaful partners, corporate clients, brokers, and affiliates), targeting a reduction in onboarding time to 1–2 days.

---

## 2. Who Uses It & Which Organizations/Users Are Involved
* **External Organization Stakeholders**: Prospective partner organizations, corporate clients, brokers, and designated Organization Admins (e.g., ABC Admin).
* **Ouch! Internal Operations & Partnership Teams**: Officers and managers evaluating organization onboarding submissions, reviewing compliance documentation, approving registrations, and maintaining partner profiles.

---

## 3. Important User Journeys

### Journey 4.1: Organization Onboarding Intake (Conceptual)
1. Prospective organization onboarding is initiated via the agreed mechanism (e.g., digital self-service registration form, invitation link generated by Ouch!, or manual back-office creation).
2. Organization information is captured: legal entity name, registration number, organization category (Insurance Partner, Corporate Client, Broker, Affiliate), primary contact details, and initial admin information.
3. Required compliance and licensing documents are uploaded (leveraging [003-document-management.md](003-document-management.md)).
4. Submission moves to `Submitted` status and enters the Ouch! operations review queue.

### Journey 4.2: Ouch! Internal Review & Approval Workflow
1. Ouch! Operations officer reviews submitted organization profile and attached verification documents.
2. Officer either:
   * Requests corrections / supplementary documents (moving status to `Pending Documents`).
   * Rejects the application with documented rationale.
   * Advances application to internal management review.
3. Upon final approval, organization status transitions to `Approved` / `Active`.
4. Organization Admin is notified and authorized to access the portal.

### Journey 4.3: Organization Profile & Agreement Governance
1. Organization Admin (e.g., ABC Admin) views and maintains company profile data, address, and primary contacts.
2. Admin accesses the agreement repository to view active contracts, terms of business, and distribution agreements tied to ABC.
3. Ouch! Operations manages partnership status, active agreements, and linked system identifiers (e.g., CRM reference ID).

---

## 4. Business Rules & Organization/Data Isolation Considerations
* **Primary Isolation Entity**: The Organization record serves as the root isolation container for all associated users, documents, agreements, and operational requests.
* **Tenant Isolation**: ABC Insurance users must never view, browse, or query other organization profiles, documents, or agreements.
* **Onboarding Efficiency**: Workflow must support the target SLA of 1–2 day turnaround from submission to operational activation.

## 7. Expected Outcome & Deliverables

* Operations review screen for reviewing, requesting rework on, and approving organization onboarding cases.
* Organization profile dashboard and agreement repository view.

---

## 8. Verification & Acceptance Checklist
- [ ] Organization onboarding submission captures entity metadata and required document attachments.
- [ ] Ouch! Operations can review, reject, request revisions, or approve organization onboarding.
- [ ] State transitions update organization status correctly (`Submitted` -> `In Review` -> `Pending Documents` -> `Approved` -> `Active`).
- [ ] External Organization Admin can view and maintain their organization's profile without seeing other organizations.
- [ ] Agreements attached to the organization are accessible in the agreement repository.
- [ ] Multi-tenant isolation verified: ABC cannot access XYZ's organization profile or agreements.
